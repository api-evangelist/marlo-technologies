# Marlo Technologies

> Marlo is a maritime ERP platform for dry bulk, tanker, and commercial shipping companies, unifying chartering, voyage management, finance, banking, and compliance in a single system. Marlo is Techstars-backed and holds SOC 2 (AICPA) certification. As of this profile the company exposes a customer web application but publishes no public developer API, API documentation, or SDKs.

## Product areas
- Voyage management: charter planning, operations tracking, cargo monitoring
- Analytics: dashboards, cashflow, valuations, credit scoring
- Finance: loan management, covenant monitoring
- Banking: multi-currency accounts, FX transfers, corporate cards
- Compliance: sanctions screening, emissions tracking, CII rating management
